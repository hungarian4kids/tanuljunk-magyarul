📍 Toldalékok (Suffixes — where is it?)

Hungarian doesn't use words like "in" or "on" the way English does — instead, a little ending attaches to the noun itself.

🎯 Tárgyeset (Direct object — the "-t" ending)

When something is on the receiving end of an action ("I see the dog"), Hungarian marks it with a -t ending.

📏 Melléknevek fokozása (Comparing adjectives)

To say something is "more" (-bb) or "the most" (leg- + -bb), Hungarian adjectives change shape too.
